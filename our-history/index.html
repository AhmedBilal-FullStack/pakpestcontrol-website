Our History

From early pest-control work to a wider range of services

The history below describes the development of our service work based on the business history and historical records available to us. Where supporting documents are available, we will add them to the relevant milestone.

2008

Service history begins

2008

Pak Pest Control's service history dates back to 2008. The earlier service work included fumigation, pest control, rodent control and spraying.

2011

Expansion into termite, waterproofing & heatproofing

2011

From 2011, termite treatment, waterproofing and heatproofing became part of the company's service work, alongside the existing pest-control and fumigation services.

2015

Historical service documentation

2015

Genuine historical business documentation from this period provides additional evidence of continuing service activity. Supporting invoice records from 2015 will be added to this section as historical references.

20s

Continued field service

2020s

The company continued providing pest-control and property-protection services while maintaining its established service range and serving customers in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and surrounding areas.

2026

Serving customers today

2026

Today, Pak Pest Control provides specialist pest-control and property-protection services for residential and commercial customers, continuing a service history that began in 2008.
